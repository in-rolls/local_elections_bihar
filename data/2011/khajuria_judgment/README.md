# Khajuria Mukhiya election, 2011

[candidates.csv](candidates.csv) records all 11 contestants and their votes from paragraph 6 of the Patna High Court judgment [Santosh Kumar Singh v. State of Bihar, 26 April 2016](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/198387463/), LPA 2142/2015. This is a manual transcription of the judgment reproduced by Indian Kanoon. The original court document has not been archived locally; direct HTTP retrieval returned 403. The source URL and paragraph references permit checking the transcription, but cannot guarantee future source availability.

Paragraphs 5–7 explicitly date this election to 2011. Paragraph 10 identifies Ara block, Bhojpur. Candidate spellings follow the vote table, including differences from the judgment's party list. Parenthetical litigation roles have been removed. `initial_outcome` means the result declared on `result_date`, not subsequent tenure: paragraph 38 upholds the later annulment of the winner's election and remands the replacement question. It does not establish that the runner-up became Mukhiya.

The initial winner matches report record `BHOJPUR_mukhiya_0021` on name, father's name, block and panchayat; [receipt.json](receipt.json) records the evidence. This supports that person's 2011 victory. It does not independently date the entire PDF or establish the date of its reservation label. Reservation is therefore not imported into this table. The winner overlaps the district-report collection; do not sum the two winner counts.

Columns: `record_id` is a local source-row key; `source_table_label` is the judgment's a–k row label; `candidate_name_raw` preserves the table's name; `votes` is an integer count; `election_year` is explicitly supported by the judgment; geography and `office` identify the contest; `initial_outcome` is elected/not_elected; `result_date` is ISO YYYY-MM-DD; `source_url` and `source_paragraph` locate each vote record. Missing values would be blank; none occur here. No candidate attributes or allegations are transcribed.

The CSV is the transcription input itself, not generated from another local table. To reproduce it, inspect paragraphs 5–7 and 10 at the source URL and transcribe the eleven rows in their printed order. CHECKSUMS fingerprints this transcription and its receipt, not the remote original. Tests check row labels, vote counts, initial winner and the cross-source identity reference.
