# Block crosswalk, 2011 report headings to 2016 blocks

`block_2011_2016.csv` maps every block heading printed in the 2011 district
Mukhiya reports to its 2016 SEC block code. It is reviewed by hand and is not
regenerated. `name_score` is the folded-name similarity that proposed the match.
Eight headings could not be proposed automatically, because they are a second
spelling of a block in the same report or add words such as "Sadar" or "Final".
These eight were assigned by review, and `note` gives the reason for each.
